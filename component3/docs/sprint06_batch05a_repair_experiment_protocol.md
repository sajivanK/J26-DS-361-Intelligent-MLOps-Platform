## 1. Executive Summary & Reviewer Corrections

Following scientific peer review of the initial Batch 5A design, this document establishes a corrected, methodologically rigorous experiment protocol. We resolve six critical scientific issues:

1. **Separation of Experimental Objectives:** We explicitly decouple **Track A (Minority-Class Augmentation)** on clean development distributions from **Track B (Controlled Concept-Drift Adaptation)** under distribution shift.
2. **Concept-Drift Repair Mechanism & Status:** We formally explain why generators trained on pre-drift positive records cannot represent shifted concepts ($P_{\text{new}}(Y \mid X)$). Because post-drift adaptation records have not been budgeted or modeled, **Track B is formally marked as BLOCKED / PREREQUISITE-GATED**. No synthetic concept labels will be fabricated or claimed as concept recovery.
3. **Authentic-Data Control Correction:** We clarify that the 262-record positive holdout belongs to the 4,931 baseline training population. For Track A, adding 200 authentic records is explicitly designated and benchmarked as an **Authentic Random OverSampling (ROS) Control**, measuring novel synthetic variance versus naive record duplication.
4. **Isolation of Random vs. Targeted Synthetic Filtering:** To prevent confounding generator architecture with filtering strategy, we define a within-generator factorial comparison (Random vs. Targeted) for both TVAE and CTGAN, strictly matching budget ($K=200$), seed, and retraining hyperparameters.
5. **Recovery Calculation Scoping:** The relative recovery fraction ($\mathcal{R}$) is strictly scoped to valid drift degradation settings with positive denominators ($\Delta F1_{\text{deg}} > 0.01$). It is explicitly marked as **not applicable** to Track A clean augmentation.
6. **Interface Verification:** Model configurations (`max_depth=10`, `n_estimators=100`), artifact paths (`component3/models/baselines/`), and serialized preprocessor structures are verified against actual codebase artifacts.

## 2. Track A: Minority-Class Augmentation (Execution-Ready for Batch 5B)

### 2.1 Research Question
> **Can targeted synthetic minority-class records—generated by deep generative models and screened via structural and empirical plausibility filters—improve downstream classification performance (Minority F1, Macro F1, PR-AUC) on an imbalanced tabular dataset over an unaugmented baseline and over naive authentic oversampling, without access to held-out test data?**

### 2.2 Testable Hypotheses
* **$H_{A1}$ (Synthetic Augmentation vs. Baseline):**  
  Augmenting the imbalanced training data with targeted synthetic records (Arms A4, A6) produces a statistically significant increase in positive-class F1 and Macro F1 on the independent clean validation partition compared to the unaugmented baseline (Arm A1):  
  $$F1_{\text{pos}}(\text{Targeted Syn}) > F1_{\text{pos}}(\text{Baseline}), \quad p < 0.05$$

* **$H_{A2}$ (Targeted Filtering vs. Random Augmentation within Generator):**  
  Within the same generator family and under an identical sample budget ($K=200$), targeted synthetic records passing Tier 2 (hard safety) and Tier 3 (empirical billing plausibility) filters outperform random synthetic records passing only Tier 2 hard safety:  
  $$F1_{\text{pos}}(\text{TVAE Targeted}) > F1_{\text{pos}}(\text{TVAE Random}), \quad p < 0.05$$  
  $$F1_{\text{pos}}(\text{CTGAN Targeted}) > F1_{\text{pos}}(\text{CTGAN Random}), \quad p < 0.05$$

* **$H_{A3}$ (Synthetic Generation vs. Authentic OverSampling Control):**  
  Targeted synthetic augmentation matches or exceeds the performance of an equal-budget authentic random oversampling control (Arm A2):  
  $$F1_{\text{pos}}(\text{Targeted Syn}) \ge F1_{\text{pos}}(\text{Authentic ROS})$$

### 2.3 Experimental Matrix for Track A ($K = 200$)

All augmentation arms add exactly $K = 200$ instances to the baseline training population ($N = 4,931 \to 5,131$):

| Arm ID | Arm Description | Base Training Instances | Augmentation Source | Added Budget ($K$) | Filtering Criteria | Primary Research Purpose |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Arm A1** | **Unaugmented Baseline** | 4,931 | None | 0 | N/A | Measures unaugmented baseline on clean validation set. |
| **Arm A2** | **Authentic OverSampling (ROS)** | 4,931 | Authentic Train Positives | 200 | Random OverSampling with replacement | Benchmark: Effect of sample duplication / weight adjustment. |
| **Arm A3** | **TVAE Random** | 4,931 | TVAE Generator | 200 | Tier 2 Hard Safety only (no plausibility screen) | Control for sample quantity in TVAE. |
| **Arm A4** | **TVAE Targeted** | 4,931 | TVAE Generator | 200 | Tier 2 Hard + Tier 3 Empirical Plausibility | Primary: Evaluates VAE-based targeted quality filtering. |
| **Arm A5** | **CTGAN Random** | 4,931 | CTGAN Generator | 200 | Tier 2 Hard Safety only (no plausibility screen) | Control for sample quantity in CTGAN. |
| **Arm A6** | **CTGAN Targeted** | 4,931 | CTGAN Generator | 200 | Tier 2 Hard + Tier 3 Empirical Plausibility | Primary: Evaluates GAN-based targeted quality filtering. |
| **Arm A7** | **TabDDPM Deduplicated** | 4,931 | TabDDPM Generator | 200 | Tier 2 Hard + Strict Deduplication | Exploratory: Evaluates diffusion repair under duplicate limits. |

### 2.4 Matched Experimental Controls (Eliminating Confounding)
1. **Factorial Comparison:** TVAE-Targeted is compared directly against TVAE-Random; CTGAN-Targeted is compared directly against CTGAN-Random. Generator architecture is never confounded with filtering strategy.
2. **Strict Budget Parity:** Every augmentation arm receives exactly $K=200$ instances.
3. **Hyperparameter Matching:** Every model is retrained from scratch using:
   - `n_estimators = 100`
   - `max_depth = 10`
   - `class_weight = 'balanced'`
   - `criterion = 'gini'`
   - `min_samples_split = 2`
   - `n_jobs = 2`
   - `random_state = 42`
4. **Evaluation Partition:** Evaluated exclusively on the independent clean validation set ($N=1,056$).
5. **Non-Claim:** Zero claim of concept-drift recovery is made from Track A.

## 3. Track B: Controlled Concept-Drift Adaptation (Blocked / Prerequisite-Gated)

### 3.1 Research Question
> **Can targeted synthetic data generation adapt a downstream classifier to a simulated structural change in the feature-label relationship ($P(Y \mid X)$), given an explicitly budgeted post-drift labeled adaptation sample, without contaminating independent evaluation partitions?**

### 3.2 Fundamental Scientific Problem with Pre-Drift Generators
Under concept drift (such as `TELCO_CONCEPT`, where customer churn is inverted for `tenure > 29.0`), the true posterior distribution changes:
$$P_{\text{post}}(Y \mid X) \ne P_{\text{pre}}(Y \mid X)$$
The generators trained in Notebook 04 (CTGAN, TVAE, TabDDPM) were fitted on the **pre-drift positive training slice** ($N=1,047$). Consequently:
1. Samples from these pre-drift generators represent the **old concept** $P_{\text{pre}}(X \mid Y=1)$.
2. Adding pre-drift synthetic records to a classifier evaluated on post-drift data injects outdated decision-boundary patterns, reinforcing the wrong relationship and accelerating performance degradation.
3. A generative model cannot infer a new conditional distribution $P(Y \mid X)$ unless it is trained or conditioned on post-drift data reflecting that new relationship.

### 3.3 Required Post-Drift Adaptation Protocol
To scientifically evaluate concept-drift adaptation, the following protocol must be satisfied:
1. **Adaptation Record Availability:** A small, explicit budget of post-drift records ($D_{\text{adapt}}$, e.g. $N_{\text{adapt}} = 200$) must be acquired (simulating audited production batches).
2. **Simulated vs. Observed Ground Truth:** In simulated benchmarks, adaptation labels are **simulated counterfactual labels** assigned by the drift injector. They must be explicitly identified as simulated labels, not observational real-world churn.
3. **Generator Fitting Permissions:** Generators must be trained or fine-tuned strictly on the post-drift adaptation records $D_{\text{adapt}}$, or conditioned on the inverted concept. Pre-drift generators cannot be used as post-drift models.
4. **Target Label Assignment:** Generated records receive target labels according to their post-drift generator conditioning, not heuristic assumption.
5. **Strict Partition Separation:** $D_{\text{adapt}}$ must be partitioned exclusively from the development scope, remaining strictly disjoint from the evaluation validation set ($N=1,056$) and test set ($N=1,056$).
6. **Abstention Condition:** **If post-drift labeled adaptation records are unavailable, the engine MUST ABSTAIN.** It must not hallucinate labels or claim concept-drift repair from pre-drift models.

### 3.4 Formal Status: BLOCKED / PREREQUISITE-GATED
Because Notebook 04 only trained generators on pre-drift data, and no post-drift adaptation slice has been budgeted or used for generator training:
* **Track B is formally marked as BLOCKED / PREREQUISITE-GATED for Batch 5.**
* No retraining for concept-drift recovery will be executed until post-drift generators are trained.
* Fabricating post-drift synthetic labels from pre-drift generators is strictly prohibited.

## 4. Strict Partition Allocation and Data-Flow Isolation

The canonical partitions established in Notebook 01 are strictly isolated:

```
[Raw Telco Churn Data: N=7,043]
   │
   ├── [Canonical Train Partition: N=4,931] (Permitted Development Scope)
   │     │
   │     ├── Authentic Positive Slice (N=1,309, Churn='Yes')
   │     │     ├── Generator Training Slice (N=1,047) ──► Fits Pre-Drift CTGAN, TVAE, TabDDPM
   │     │     └── Internal Holdout Slice (N=262) ──► Provides Pool for Track A Authentic ROS (K=200)
   │     │
   │     └── Authentic Negative Slice (N=3,622, Churn='No')
   │
   ├── [Canonical Validation Partition: N=1,056] (Evaluation Scope)
   │     ├── Clean Validation (N=1,056) ──► Evaluates Track A Augmentation & Catastrophic Forgetting
   │     └── Drifted Validation (N=1,056) ──► Reserved for Track B Evaluation (TELCO_CONCEPT)
   │
   └── [Canonical Test Partition: N=1,056] (Locked Scope)
         └── STRICTLY UNTOUCHED (Permanently unobserved during development)
```

### Partition Safeguards:
1. **Duplicate-Safe Internal Split:** The 1,309 positive training records are partitioned into 1,047 generator training records and 262 internal holdout records using atomic group-aware splitting. **Zero identical 19-feature records cross this boundary.**
2. **Preprocessor Fitting Isolation:** The tabular preprocessor (`TabularDataPreprocessor`) is fitted **strictly on the 4,931 baseline training instances**. No validation or synthetic data is ever seen during preprocessor fitting.
3. **Outer Partition Disjointness:** $Train \cap Val = \emptyset$, $Train \cap Test = \emptyset$, $Val \cap Test = \emptyset$.

## 5. Fair Retraining Strategy

1. **Retraining from Scratch:**  
   Scikit-learn's `RandomForestClassifier` does not support incremental learning or `partial_fit`. All augmented models are refitted from scratch on $D_{\text{train}} \cup D_{\text{aug}}$.
2. **Terminology Discipline:**  
   This procedure is explicitly documented as **"retraining from scratch on augmented tabular data"**, never mischaracterized as incremental learning.
3. **Hyperparameter Matching:**  
   All arms use the exact configuration of the frozen baseline:
   - `n_estimators = 100`
   - `max_depth = 10`
   - `class_weight = 'balanced'`
   - `criterion = 'gini'`
   - `min_samples_split = 2`
   - `n_jobs = 2`
   - `random_state = 42`
4. **Artifact Preservation:**  
   The baseline model (`component3/models/baselines/telco_churn_random_forest.joblib`) remains frozen. Repaired models are saved to:  
   `component3/models/repair/telco_churn_rf_track_a_arm_{1..7}.joblib`.

## 6. Evaluation Metrics & Statistical Evaluation Plan

### 6.1 Track A Metrics (Clean Minority Augmentation)
Evaluated on the independent clean validation partition ($N=1,056$):
* **Minority-Class F1 ($F1_{\text{pos}}$), Precision ($P$), Recall ($R$).**
* **Macro F1 ($F1_{\text{macro}}$):** Unweighted average of positive and negative class F1.
* **Area Under ROC Curve ($AUC$).**
* **Area Under Precision-Recall Curve ($PR\text{-}AUC$).**
* **Absolute Metric Delta:**
  $$\Delta F1 = F1_{\text{augmented}} - F1_{\text{baseline}}$$
* **Relative Percentage Gain:**
  $$\% \text{ Gain} = \frac{F1_{\text{augmented}} - F1_{\text{baseline}}}{F1_{\text{baseline}}} \times 100$$
* **Recovery Fraction:** **NOT APPLICABLE** to Track A (clean unshifted distribution; denominator is 0).

### 6.2 Track B Metrics (Concept Drift Adaptation - When Unblocked)
* **Degradation Magnitude:** $\Delta F1_{\text{deg}} = F1_{\text{clean}} - F1_{\text{degraded}}$.
* **Recovery Fraction ($\mathcal{R}$):**
  $$\mathcal{R} = \frac{F1_{\text{repaired}} - F1_{\text{degraded}}}{F1_{\text{clean}} - F1_{\text{degraded}}}$$
  - **Singularity Guardrail:** Evaluated **only if $\Delta F1_{\text{deg}} > 0.01$**. If $\Delta F1_{\text{deg}} \le 0.01$, $\mathcal{R}$ is flagged as `UNDEFINED_ZERO_DEGRADATION`.
* **Catastrophic Forgetting Retention Check:**
  $$\text{Retention Ratio} = \frac{F1_{\text{clean}}(\text{Repaired})}{F1_{\text{clean}}(\text{Baseline})} \ge 0.95$$

### 6.3 Uncertainty Estimation & Statistical Testing
* **1,000 Paired Bootstrap Resamples:** 95% confidence intervals derived from percentiles of bootstrap metric deltas ($\Delta F1$).
* **Permutation Test:** Paired permutation test with $B=1,000$ iterations testing $H_0: \Delta F1 \le 0$. Results are reported with exact $p$-values. No difference is declared statistically significant without $p < 0.05$.

## 7. Leakage Risks and Mitigations Matrix

| Identified Leakage Risk | Severity | Specific Mitigation Protocol |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Validation/Test Leakage into Generator** | Critical | Generator fit strictly on $D_{\text{gen}} \subset D_{\text{train}}$ ($N=1,047$). Verified by automated disjoint test. |
| **Duplicate Crossing in Internal Split** | High | Atomic grouping assigns all identical 19-feature records to one partition. Overlap verified as exactly 0. |
| **Preprocessor Contamination** | High | Preprocessor fit strictly on baseline training partition ($N=4,931$). Augmentation data transformed using frozen scaler. |
| **Label Snooping in Augmentation Selection** | Critical | Synthetic records selected strictly via unsupervised structural & physical checks ($P(X)$), never evaluation labels. |
| **Pre-Drift Generator Reuse under Concept Drift** | Critical | Pre-drift generators strictly prohibited from concept-drift repair. Track B blocked until post-drift generators exist. |
| **Hyperparameter Tuning on Test Set** | Critical | Test partition ($N=1,056$) remains permanently unobserved during development. |

## 9. Failure Conditions & Experiment Acceptance Criteria

### Experiment Failure Conditions (Triggers Rejection):
1. **Catastrophic Forgetting:** Clean validation Macro F1 drops by $> 5\%$ after augmentation.
2. **Sample Insufficiency:** Any generator fails to yield $K=200$ valid unique records.
3. **Partition Leakage:** Any overlap detected between training, holdout, validation, or test records.
4. **Fabricated Recovery:** Any claim of concept-drift recovery without a post-drift adaptation generator.

### Experiment Acceptance Criteria:
1. Targeted synthetic augmentation (Arm A4 or A6) produces a statistically significant improvement in clean validation Minority F1 over Arm A1 ($\Delta F1 > 0$, $p < 0.05$).
2. Within-generator targeted filtering outperforms random filtering ($Arm\ A4 > Arm\ A3$ and $Arm\ A6 > Arm\ A5$).
3. All metrics, models, and manifests serialize without error.
