## 1. Executive Summary & Scientific Purpose

The core objective of Sprint 06 Batch 5K is to empirically determine whether adding synthetic post-drift records generated by deep tabular generative models (**TVAE**, **CTGAN**, or **TabDDPM**) yields a measurable downstream classification advantage over:
1. **Unadapted source baseline ($B0$):** The frozen historical model trained strictly on $D_{\text{source,train}}$ ($N=3,452$).
2. **Naive authentic adaptation ($B1\text{-}B200$):** Naive unweighted pooling of $D_{\text{source,train}}$ ($N=3,452$) with the 200 authentic post-drift records ($B200$).
3. **Authentic Random OverSampling ($B2\text{-}B200\text{-}K200$):** Retraining on $D_{\text{source,train}} \cup B200$ with 200 authentic duplicated records drawn uniformly from $B200$.
4. **Target-only adaptation ($B4\text{-}B200$):** Retraining strictly on $B200$ ($N=200$).

This is a **controlled, exploratory development experiment**. Under strict MLOps scientific integrity principles:
* **No Real-World Recovery Claims:** The experiment evaluates response under the simulated `TELCO_CONCEPT` distribution shift. It does not claim real-world market drift recovery.
* **Zero Target Evaluation Contamination:** The locked evaluation dataset $D_{\text{target,eval}}$ ($N=493$) is strictly isolated and **forbidden** during this batch. All primary adaptation utility comparisons are conducted on the independent post-drift development validation partition $D_{\text{dev\_val}}$ ($N=292$).
* **Matched Model Architecture:** All downstream models use the identical, frozen Random Forest configuration.
* **Fixed Classification Threshold:** The standard 0.5 decision threshold is held invariant across all models. Threshold search on $D_{\text{dev\_val}}$ is strictly forbidden.
* **Honest Negative Reporting:** If synthetic data augmentation fails to outperform authentic Random OverSampling ($B2$), this negative result will be formally reported without retroactive modification of hyperparameters, filtering criteria, or scoring metrics.

## 4. Candidate Eligibility Policy & Multi-Tier Screening

Every candidate synthetic record undergoes strict sequential and independent filtering:

1. **Tier 1 (Structural Integrity):**
   - Correct 20-column schema matching the dataset registry.
   - Zero `NaN`, `None`, or infinite values in any feature.
   - All categorical values within valid domain sets observed in $B200$.
   - Target variable `Churn` strictly in `{"No", "Yes"}`.
2. **Tier 2 (Hard Physical Domain Constraints):**
   - `tenure` $\in [0.0, 72.0]$.
   - `MonthlyCharges` $\ge 0.0$.
   - `TotalCharges` $\ge 0.0$.
3. **Full-Feature Deduplication:**
   - Removal of exact intra-sample duplicates.
4. **Exact Training-Record Match Screening:**
   - Candidates identical across all 20 columns to any authentic $B200$ record are flagged and discarded (memorization check).
5. **Forbidden Feature Signature Check:**
   - 19-feature string signatures checked against $D_{\text{dev\_val}}$, $D_{\text{source\_eval}}$, and $D_{\text{target\_eval}}$. Any cross-partition feature identity is discarded.
6. **Tier 3 (Empirical Plausibility):**
   - Billing ratio screen: $\frac{\text{TotalCharges}}{\max(1, \text{tenure}) \times \text{MonthlyCharges}} \in [0.5, 1.5]$.
   - Records outside this physical range represent ungrounded generative artifacts and are excluded from training pools.
7. **Privacy Risk Screening:**
   - Minimum Euclidean Distance to Closest Record (DCR) on min-max normalized continuous features. Near-duplicates ($\text{DCR} < 0.01$) are audited.

### Label Governance Rules
- Generated labels are preserved strictly as emitted by the generator fitted on $B200$.
- No synthetic records are relabeled using the hidden `TELCO_CONCEPT` programmatic rule.
- No ground-truth labels from $D_{\text{dev\_val}}$ or evaluation records are used to adjust synthetic labels.

## 7. Primary & Secondary Evaluation Protocol

### Primary Evaluation: Adaptation Utility
- **Evaluation Split:** $D_{\text{dev\_val}}$ ($N=292$, post-drift concept truth).
- **Core Metrics:**
  - Positive-class F1 score (`Churn="Yes"`, threshold = 0.5)
  - Macro F1 score
  - Balanced Accuracy
  - Precision & Recall
  - Area Under Precision-Recall Curve (PR-AUC / Average Precision)
  - Area Under ROC Curve (ROC-AUC)
  - Confusion Matrix ($TN, FP, FN, TP$)

### Secondary Evaluation: Source Retention Diagnostic
- **Evaluation Split:** $D_{\text{source,eval}}$ ($N=493$, pre-drift observational truth).
- **Retention Trade-off Rule:** Adaptation to a shifted concept frequently causes catastrophic forgetting of original concept rules. A synthetic arm cannot be called successful if marginal gains on $D_{\text{dev\_val}}$ coincide with severe performance collapse on $D_{\text{source,eval}}$.

---

## 8. Statistical Uncertainty & Multiple Comparison Controls

All pairwise differences $\Delta \text{F1} = \text{F1}_{\text{candidate}} - \text{F1}_{\text{reference}}$ are computed on identical, paired evaluation predictions:
1. **Paired Bootstrap CIs:** $B = 2,000$ bootstrap resamples, 95% two-sided percentile interval, fixed seed 42.
2. **Paired Permutation Tests:** $R = 2,000$ sign-flip permutations (or exact two-sided permutation p-value), fixed seed 42.
3. **Multiplicity Adjustment:** Step-down Holm-Bonferroni correction applied across the family of hypotheses ($S1, S2, S3$ vs $B0$, $B1$, and $B2$).

---

## 9. Decision Criteria & Scientific Integrity Guardrails

To determine the status of synthetic tabular repair under concept drift:
- **SUPERIORITY CRITERIA:** A synthetic arm $S_i$ is considered demonstrably superior if and only if:
  $$\text{F1}(S_i) > \text{F1}(B2\text{-}B200\text{-}K200) \quad \text{with} \quad p_{\text{adj}} < 0.05 \quad \text{and lower CI bound} > 0.0$$
- **EQUIVALENCE / INFERIORITY:** If $\text{F1}(S_i) \le \text{F1}(B2\text{-}B200\text{-}K200)$ or the difference fails to achieve statistical significance, the finding is formally documented as **no synthetic advantage over authentic Random OverSampling**.
- **UNCONDITIONAL TRANSPARENCY:** No post-hoc manipulation of thresholds, no selective reporting, and no cherry-picking of alternative metrics will be conducted to fabricate a positive outcome.
